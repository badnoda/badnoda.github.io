I am an Assistant Professor at the Department of Mechanical Engineering, Graduate School of Engineering, University of Hyogo, where I lead the **Tsunoda Lab (Field Swarm Robotics)**.
My research aims to build swarm robot systems that keep working in harsh, unstructured environments: control design of group robots inspired by sheepdog shepherding, collective behavior of swarm systems, and multi-legged / bio-inspired robots for disaster response in unknown environments.

*Last updated: November 2026 (synchronized with [researchmap](https://researchmap.jp/badnoda)). English pages: [Research](/en/research/) · [CV](/en/cv/) / 日本語: [研究](/research/) · [業績](/cv/)*

- **Book (2026):** 『Pythonで始めるスワーム制御プログラミング』 (Swarm control programming with Python), Corona Publishing, Feb. 2026 (ISBN 9784339032499).
- **Recent journal papers:** *Robotics and Autonomous Systems* (2026, lead/corresponding author), *Advanced Robotics* (2026, lead/corresponding author), *IFAC Journal of Systems and Control* (2026).
- **Research projects:** Member of a JST *Economic Security Program* (経済安全保障重要技術育成プログラム, K Program) project on guidance and collision avoidance of small UAV swarms (2025–2029); PI of JSPS KAKENHI Grant-in-Aid for Early-Career Scientists (2025–2028) on swarm robot navigation in unspecified environments.
- **Awards (2025–2026):** ICAMechS 2026 Best Paper Award; SICE Control Division Multi-Symposium Award (2026); two SI2025 Excellent Presentation Awards; ICICIC 2025 Best Presentation Award.
- **Media:** Prototype disaster-response robots for river-channel blockage (landslide dams), developed with collaborators, were covered by NHK and several newspapers (Nov.–Dec. 2025).
- **Track record:** 46 peer-reviewed papers and 95 presentations listed on researchmap (as of Nov. 2026).
